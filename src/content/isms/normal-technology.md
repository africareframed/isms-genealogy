Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor see AI as transformative but normal, like electricity, spreading over decades. On that view both superintelligence fears and utopian hype are misplaced.

Economists supply its history. Electricity took decades to show up in productivity, and in 1987 Robert Solow saw the computer age everywhere but in the statistics. In 2024 Daron Acemoglu put AI's boost to productivity over ten years at well under one per cent.
