The movement defends their work, their children, their dignity and their democratic say against a race to replace humans and concentrated corporate power. It wants humans kept in charge of AI, children shielded from manipulative chatbots, and AI companies held liable for harm. It is not against technology, its leaders say, but for AI tools that serve people.

Its main convener, the Future of Life Institute, came from the risk camp. Founded in 2014 to study existential risk, it led the 2023 pause letter and the 2025 call to ban superintelligence. In 2026 it brought unions, faith groups, parents, progressives and conservatives to one declaration, and put Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon on one stage.

Other strands are older. Critics of social media, such as the Center for Humane Technology, and family groups fighting Big Tech turned from smartphones to chatbots. Faith leaders joined too, and in October 2026 Pope Leo XIV addressed a meeting the institute co-organised with the Pontifical Academy for Life.
