The fear is old: Samuel Butler, Alan Turing, Norbert Wiener and I. J. Good all warned that machines might escape human control. From 2006, Eliezer Yudkowsky's essays on bias, probability and AI drew a community to Overcoming Bias, then LessWrong. Its members call themselves rationalists; critics often call them doomers.

For two decades the aim was to solve alignment first: to give a future superintelligence goals that are safe for people. After ChatGPT, MIRI and others concluded that this would not happen in time. The call to pause followed: open letters in 2023, then demands for a treaty banning superintelligence, and marches outside the labs.
