Norbert Wiener warned from 1950 that automatic machines could cause mass unemployment. Joseph Weizenbaum held in 1976 that some choices belong to humans alone. From 2014, researchers and journalists showed how scoring systems and face recognition treat groups unequally.

Since 2021 the movement has studied the labour, data and power behind large language models; leading voices call extinction fears a distraction. Its normal-technology strand, from Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor, sees AI as transformative but normal, like electricity, spreading over decades. On that view both superintelligence fears and utopian hype are misplaced.
