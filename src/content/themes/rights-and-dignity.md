First signers included the UK, the US, Israel and the European Union, which ratified it in May 2026.

The words are older than AI. “All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights,” runs Article 1 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948). European institutions and religious leaders now apply that language to AI.

The isms mostly start elsewhere: with survival, with the welfare of future generations, or with the freedom to remake oneself. Successionists ask a further question: whether rights should stop at the human at all.
