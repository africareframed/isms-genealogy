    source: "Paul Christiano, Jan Leike and others, “Deep reinforcement learning from human preferences”, arXiv:1706.03741, 12 June 2017; Long Ouyang and others, “Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback”, arXiv:2203.02155, 4 March 2022; Will Douglas Heaven, “ChatGPT is OpenAI's latest fix for GPT-3. The worry is older than the word. In 1960 Norbert Wiener warned that we must be sure “the purpose put into the machine is the purpose which we really desire”. Eliezer Yudkowsky called the goal Friendly AI in 2001, and Nick Bostrom's Superintelligence (2014) framed it as the control problem.

The word itself took hold in late 2014. That November Stuart Russell wrote of “the value alignment problem”, and MIRI's research agenda, published in December, swapped Friendly AI for “aligned”. MIRI's Rob Bensinger recalls that Russell suggested the term, but Russell is reported to have said he did not coin it.
