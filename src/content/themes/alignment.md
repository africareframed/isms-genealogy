The worry is older than the word. In 1960 Norbert Wiener warned that we must be sure “the purpose put into the machine is the purpose which we really desire”. Eliezer Yudkowsky called the goal Friendly AI in 2001, and Nick Bostrom's Superintelligence (2014) framed it as the control problem.

The word itself came into use in late 2014, when Stuart Russell wrote of “the value alignment problem”. That December, Yudkowsky's institute, MIRI, swapped Friendly AI for “aligned” in its research agenda. Who coined the term is uncertain, though MIRI's Rob Bensinger recalls that Russell suggested it to MIRI that year.
