Critique is the outside voice. It comes from AI ethics researchers, economists and computer scientists who do not share the isms' starting points. They ask who is harmed now, who gains, and how fast change really comes.

Some critics name the isms directly. Émile P. Torres, working with Timnit Gebru, coined TESCREAL and brought it into public debate in 2023. The acronym joins seven ideologies and takes in four of the isms mapped here.
