American Dynamism

Includes a national AGI project, AI dominance and the technological republic

American Dynamism holds that AI decides national power, and that America and its allies must out-build China to set the rules.

Katherine Boyle of Andreessen Horowitz coined the name and made its case in 2022: start-ups building for American strength, from defence to factories. Palantir’s Alex Karp argues that Silicon Valley grew on Pentagon money in the Cold War and should rebuild its ties with government.

Its roots are older: Alexander Hamilton’s case for American manufactures, the wartime “arsenal of democracy” and the Manhattan Project. Since 2024 its national strand has pressed for an AGI project of its own, and in 2025 the White House made “global AI dominance” its policy.

Who holds it now

Andreessen Horowitz's American Dynamism practice

Founded in 2022 by Katherine Boyle and David Ulevitch to back companies that “support the national interest”, from defence and aerospace to manufacturing. Its portfolio includes Anduril, Shield AI and Skydio.

Alex Karp

Chief executive of Palantir. In The Technological Republic (2025), with Nicholas Zamiska, he argues that Silicon Valley's engineers have a duty to take part in the nation's defence.

Anduril

Defence company founded in 2017 by Palmer Luckey, the founder of Oculus. It builds autonomous systems for the military, and in 2025 it was valued at $30.5 billion.

Leopold Aschenbrenner

Former OpenAI researcher. His essay Situational Awareness (2024) calls for a national project to build AGI before China, and he runs an investment fund of the same name.

Special Competitive Studies Project

Non-profit set up by Eric Schmidt in 2021 to carry on the work of the National Security Commission on AI. It argues that America must win the technology competition with China.

David Sacks

Investor and the White House's AI and crypto czar since 2025. He argues that the United States must win the AI race with China.

Promises

A national-security state fused with its tech industry, in a world divided into technology blocs. It echoes Hamilton’s national development and the wartime “arsenal of democracy”.

If American Dynamism’s demands became law tomorrow, AI would become a defence priority. Export controls and alliances would harden, and the world would split into American and Chinese stacks.

Interests

Venture capital funds it, more than philanthropy. Andreessen Horowitz raised $600 million for its American Dynamism practice in April 2024. Founders Fund put $1 billion into Anduril’s $2.5 billion round in June 2025, at a valuation of $30.5 billion.

Leopold Aschenbrenner’s Situational Awareness fund started in September 2024 with about $225 million of seed capital, from Patrick and John Collison, Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross among others.

Its demands could serve those who build for the state. Labs and cloud firms with government contracts gain, since national projects run through chosen contractors. Defence-tech start-ups gain as the race opens defence budgets to new firms, and defence contractors and energy firms gain from rearmament and the build-out.

Investors in defence technology could see their portfolios turned into policy, and China hawks in both parties gain a cause that unites them. Universities get a mixed deal: more defence research money, but limits on foreign students and collaboration.

Its opponents could gain as well. A race justifies security budgets on both sides, so the security establishments of its rivals gain too.

Winning the Race

The White House

The White House publishes America's AI Action Plan under the title Winning the Race. It sets out federal actions to speed up AI adoption, build data centres and export American AI to allies. The contest with China is framed as a race the United States must win.

The White House, “Winning the Race. America's AI Action Plan”, July 2025

The Technological Republic

Alexander C. Karp and Nicholas W. Zamiska

Palantir's chief executive and its head of corporate affairs argue that Silicon Valley turned away from the state to build consumer software. They call on engineers to work with government again on defence and national purpose, as their predecessors did in the Cold War.

Alexander C. Karp and Nicholas W. Zamiska, The Technological Republic. Hard Power, Soft Belief, and the Future of the West, Crown, 2025

“Global AI dominance”

President Donald Trump

Days after revoking his predecessor's order on AI safety, the president signs a new one. It makes it American policy to sustain and enhance the country's global AI dominance. The phrase gives the movement's national strand its name in government.

Executive Order 14179, “Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence”, 23 January 2025

A Manhattan Project for AGI

U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission

The congressional commission's annual report puts one recommendation first. Congress should set up and fund a programme, like the Manhattan Project, dedicated to racing to artificial general intelligence.

U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission, 2024 Annual Report to Congress, November 2024

Situational Awareness

Leopold Aschenbrenner

A former OpenAI researcher publishes a long essay arguing that AGI could arrive by 2027. He calls for a government-led national project to build it first and secure it against China. The essay became a founding text for the idea of a national AGI project.

Leopold Aschenbrenner, “Situational Awareness. The Decade Ahead”, situational-awareness.ai, June 2024

A $600 million fund

Andreessen Horowitz

Andreessen Horowitz raises $600 million for its American Dynamism practice, as part of $7.2 billion in new funds. The money goes to start-ups in defence, aerospace, manufacturing and other areas it calls the national interest.

Andreessen Horowitz, “New Funds, New Era”, a16z, April 2024

2022MilestoneDraft · to be checked

Export controls on AI chips

U.S. Department of Commerce

The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security restricts exports to China of the most advanced AI chips and the equipment to make them. The rules turn computing power into an instrument of national strategy. Later rounds widened them.

Bureau of Industry and Security, U.S. Department of Commerce, rules on advanced computing and semiconductor manufacturing items, 7 October 2022

2022Modern definition

Building American Dynamism

Katherine Boyle and David Ulevitch of Andreessen Horowitz

On 14 January 2022 Andreessen Horowitz published the thesis of its American Dynamism practice. The firm had used the name in 2021 but left it largely undefined. Boyle's essay argued that only technologists building companies for the national interest could end stagnation, in fields from national security to housing. The firm already backed defence start-ups such as Anduril and Shield AI.

Katherine Boyle, “Building American Dynamism”, a16z, 14 January 2022; David Ulevitch and Katherine Boyle, “Investing in American Dynamism”, a16z, 14 January 2022

The National Security Commission on AI

Eric Schmidt and the commission

A commission set up by Congress and chaired by Google's former chief executive delivers its final report. It warns that America is not prepared to defend or compete in the AI era, and calls for a national effort to win the technology competition with China.

National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence, Final Report, March 2021

2018Reacted againstDraft · to be checked

Google leaves Project Maven

Google employees

Thousands of Google employees protest the company's work on Project Maven, a Pentagon programme using AI to analyse drone footage. Google says it will not renew the contract. The episode became the movement's example of Silicon Valley turning its back on national defence.

Scott Shane and Daisuke Wakabayashi, “‘The Business of War’: Google Employees Protest Work for the Pentagon”, The New York Times, 4 April 2018

China's plan to lead in AI

China's State Council

China's government publishes its New Generation Artificial Intelligence Development Plan. It aims to make China the world's primary centre of AI innovation by 2030. For the movement it marks the start of the race it says America must win.

State Council of the People's Republic of China, New Generation Artificial Intelligence Development Plan, July 2017 (translation by New America's DigiChina)

Anduril

Palmer Luckey and co-founders

The founder of Oculus starts a defence company built like a software start-up, with partners from Founders Fund and Palantir. Anduril sells autonomous systems and surveillance towers to governments, and became the model of the new defence start-up.

1945Attributed by historiansDraft · to be checked

Science, the Endless Frontier

Vannevar Bush

The wartime head of American research sends the president a plan for science in peacetime. Public money, he argues, should fund basic research for health, jobs and national security. The report shaped the alliance of state, universities and industry that built Silicon Valley.

Vannevar Bush, Science, the Endless Frontier, report to the President, July 1945

The Manhattan Project

The United States government

The United States sets up a secret programme to build the atomic bomb before Nazi Germany. Scientists, the army and industry work under one command. Its advocates in AI hold it up as the model for a national AGI project.

U.S. Department of Energy, The Manhattan Project. An Interactive History; Leopold Aschenbrenner, “Situational Awareness”, 2024

1940Attributed by historiansDraft · to be checked

The arsenal of democracy

Franklin D. Roosevelt

In a radio address the president asks American industry to supply the countries fighting Nazi Germany. “We must be the great arsenal of democracy,” he says. The idea of industry as the nation's defence returns in the movement's case for building at home.

Franklin D. Roosevelt, fireside chat on national security, 29 December 1940

Before 1800

1791Attributed by historiansDraft · to be checked

Report on Manufactures

Alexander Hamilton

The first Treasury secretary argues that the young republic should protect and promote its own industry, not rely on imports. National strength, he holds, depends on what a country can make. It became a founding text of what economists call the American School.

Alexander Hamilton, Report on the Subject of Manufactures, 5 December 1791
